## Tuning

`llama bench` in the same image, `-p 512 -n 128`, two repetitions, tok/s:

| threads | QAD-Q4_0 pp512 | QAD-Q4_0 tg128 | Q4_K_M tg128 | Q8_0 tg128 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 256 | 35.7 | 26.3 | 19.7 |
| 6 | 360 | 41.5 | 33.9 | 23.8 |
| 8 | 421 | 47.7 | 39.2 | 28.5 |
| 12 | 500 | 51.6 | 45.3 | 30.1 |
| 16 | 513 | 44.6 | 39.3 | 25.5 |
| 24 | 567 | 42.3 | 33.2 | 22.5 |

QAD-Q4_0 is the fastest file at every thread count. Twelve threads decode 8% faster than eight here (with a ±2.9
spread), but the launch would then demand 12 threads (`needs.cpus`) of every machine, and above 12 decode falls;
eight is the launch.

Decode at depth, QAD-Q4_0, eight threads, one repetition:

| flash attention | pp2048 @ 8K | tg64 @ 8K | pp2048 @ 32K | tg64 @ 32K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| off | 278 | 40.2 | 156 | 16.8 |
| on | 326 | 40.0 | 183 | 10.2 |

Flash attention on the CPU prefills 15-18% faster but decodes 40% slower at 32K, so it is off.

## What failed

- **262,144 (the model's full window), flash attention on:** loads and passes chat, reasoning and tools, but the
  context gate's 184,717-token prompt took 2,099 s to prefill (88 tok/s average, 38 s per 2,048-token batch at the
  end), and the answer then decoded at 2.5 tok/s; it had thought for over 700 tokens when I stopped it. Not a usable
  CPU window, so no recipe at that size.
