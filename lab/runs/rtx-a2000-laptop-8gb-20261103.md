# RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU: Gemma 4 12B, Bonsai 2 9B and Qwen3.6 35B-A3B

> Tuning notes, in the order the work happened. Many recipes, launches, figures and `needs` values here were replaced
> later. The recipes in this PR and their final figures are in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.md`.

## Hardware and runtime

- Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 5, Intel Core i7-12700H, 32 GB DDR5-4800 (one module)
- NVIDIA RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU, sm86, default 35 W power limit, no power or clock changes
- Omarchy 4.0.6, kernel 7.2.15, NVIDIA driver 615.78.08, Docker 29.9.2, NVIDIA Container Toolkit 1.20.1 (CDI)

`rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261103.probe.json` is the recipe kit's `tools/probe.py` output. The
`rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261103.memwatch-*.csv` files are `tools/memwatch.sh` logs, one per run.

## Method

The Local AI gateway returns 404 for `/v1/token/encode`, `/tokenize` and `/v1/tokenize`, so the lab can't count
tokens through it. Each engine was started with `docker run` as rendered by `lab.py try --dry-run`, with its port
published on `127.0.0.1`. The Qwen3.6 35B-A3B container was also capped at 27 GB with `--memory` and
`--memory-swap`.
