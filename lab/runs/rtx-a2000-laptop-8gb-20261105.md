# RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU: faster MoE launches and Welp-35B-A3B

> Tuning notes, in the order the work happened. Many recipes, launches, figures and `needs` values here were replaced
> later. The recipes in this PR and their final figures are in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.md`.

Hardware, driver and method are as in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261103.md`. Every engine container was capped at 27 GB
(`--memory`, `--memory-swap`). `tools/memwatch.sh` recorded only timestamps on this laptop (`bc` was not installed), so its logs are left out; RAM figures come from the lab runner's MemAvailable readings in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.runlabs.log`.

## Qwen3.6 35B-A3B and Ornith 1.5 35B-A3B

The new launches change four things from the 20261104 A2000 launches: llama.cpp b11681, a q4_0 KV cache (the smaller
cache lets auto-fit put more experts on the card), `--fit-target 256`, and the CPU share on the 12 performance-core
threads (`--threads 12 --cpu-range 0-11 --cpu-strict 1`; the i7-12700H's E-cores slow the expert work down). MTP draft
stays at 1.

30 s decode runs (tok/s, mean of two or three samples):

| Variant | Qwen3.6 | Ornith 1.5 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 20261104 A2000 launch (b11146) | 24.8 | 28.1 |
| image b11681 | 26.0 | 27.9 |
| q4_0 KV | 28.5 | 29.5 |
| q4_0 KV, b11681 | 28.9 | 30.2 |
| q4_0 KV, 12 P-core threads | 29.6 | 31.3 |
| q4_0 KV, 6 P-core threads | 28.5 | 31.1 |
| q4_0 KV, fit 256 | 29.6 | 30.8 |
| q4_0 KV, ubatch 1024 | 28.5 | 29.1 |
| q4_0 KV, MTP draft 2 | | 27.3 |
| q4_0 KV, b11681, 12 P-core threads, fit 256 | 30.4 | 31.3 |
| same, ubatch 1024 | 29.4 | 30.7 |

| Recipe | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|

Both recipes move to the new launches (25.2 and 27.8 tok/s before). `needs`: 23 GB RAM and 12 CPU threads for Qwen,
22 GB and 12 for Ornith. The 20261104 A2000 launches are removed; nothing uses them.

## Welp-35B-A3B

Quaedra's 13.2 GB GGUF of a Qwen3.6-35B-A3B tune, no MTP head. The A2000 launch is the rtx-5060-ti-16gb auto-fit
launch with b11681, `--fit-target 256`, the 12 P-core threads and ubatch 512, at the full 262,144 tokens with a q4_0 KV
cache.

| Variant (30 s runs) | Decode (tok/s) | Prefill (tok/s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| rtx-5060-ti-16gb launch (fit 1024) | 30.5 | 228 |
| fit 512 / fit 256 | 31.4 / 31.5 | 235 / 238 |
| fit 512, ubatch 512 | 31.2 | 363 |
| fit 512, weights loaded without mmap | 31.4 | 272 |
| fit 512, b11681 | 31.7 | 248 |
| fit 256, 12 P-core threads | 33.1 | 235 |
| fit 256, 6 P-core threads | 33.2 | 234 |
| fit 256, 12 P-core threads, ubatch 512 | 33.0 | 363 |
| fit 256, 12 P-core threads, b11681 | 33.7 | 250 |

| Recipe | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |

| `welp-35b-a3b.llama.cpp.256k` | all six | 33.0 tok/s | 272 tok/s | 1.5 GB |

The weights are memory-mapped, so free RAM falls by only 1.5 GB, but the experts that stay off the card live in the
page cache: loaded without mmap the drop is 8.7 GB. `needs.host_ram_gb` is 10 so that share stays in RAM.

## Qwen3.6 35B-A3B on SGLang MoE offload

yeasah's 3.08 bpw EXL3 on the sglang-exl3-flashnext image (trellis-serve MoE offload: routed experts in pinned host RAM,
streamed into a GPU expert cache). Start point: the `rtx-3060-ti-8gb` launch.

| Variant (30 s runs) | Decode (tok/s) | Prefill (tok/s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| rtx-3060-ti-8gb launch, 128K / 262,144 | 24.7 / 24.7 | 1,403 / 1,409 |
| mem fraction 0.90 | 24.7 | out of memory in prefill |
| CPU expert mode (`SGLANG_EXL3_MOE_OFFLOAD=cpu`), 12 / 8 threads | 20.1 / 16.0 | 1,028 / 1,016 |
| NEXTN MTP 1 / 2 / 3, mem 0.80 | 26.6 / 25.7 / 24.5 | out of memory in prefill |
| MTP 1, mem 0.75 | 26.4 | 1,363 |
| MTP 2, mem 0.80, chunked prefill 2048 | 25.6 | 1,209 |
| MTP 1, mem 0.80, chunked prefill 2048 | 26.8 | 1,206 |

The checkpoint's MTP layer needs `--speculative-token-map` with an EXL3 target (the draft gets no LM head otherwise);
the image ships `/opt/sglang-exl3/tokenmaps/qwen36_hot32k_v2.pt`. At mem 0.75 the KV pool holds 203,459 tokens, too few
for the context gate at 262,144 (it fills about 85%); at 0.80 it holds about 230K.

| Recipe | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `qwen3.6-35b-a3b.sglang.256k` | all six | 27.0 tok/s | 476 tok/s | 19.0 GB |

Prefill is over the context gate's 223K-token prompt. llama.cpp stays faster for decode (30.0 tok/s), and SGLang prefills
about five times faster on short prompts (1,206 against 225 tok/s at 16K).

## Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B

Unsloth's UD-Q3_K_XL GGUF, b11681, 12 P-core threads, ubatch 1024. Auto-fit leaves most of it in RAM. The model's context is 1M tokens, but long prompts stop it answering in the reply.

### No recipe

- LFM3-Omni-2B Q4_0 at 128,000: 116.3 and 115.6 tok/s, failed the reasoning gate in both labs. The gate requires thinking output and the model card says the model is non-reasoning.
- LFM2.5-2.6B 72K passed a lab at 81.4 tok/s, but the code came back in only 2 of 4 context probes, so the 64K recipe is used.

### Qwen3.8-27B: no recipe on this card

None of these pass. Decode is 35 W compute bound, and the only file fast enough is too weak.

| Setup | Decode | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| UD-IQ1_S + separate MTP file, draft 1, 16K | 15.8, 14.4, 17.8 tok/s | Three labs failed: tools; reasoning, context, speed; context |
| UD-IQ1_S + MTP, 24K | 14.5 tok/s | Lab failed: tools, context, speed |
| UD-IQ1_S + MTP, 32K | 16.6 tok/s | Lab failed: chat |
| UD-IQ1_M + MTP, 8K / 12K | 14.8 / 14.6 tok/s | Sweep, under 15; 16K crashes |
| UD-IQ2_XXS, 16K | 11.1 tok/s | Sweep, under 15 |
| UD-IQ2_XXS + MTP on the GPU | none | Out of memory at 4K, 8K and 16K |
| UD-IQ2_XXS + MTP on the CPU | 12.4 tok/s | Sweep, under 15 |
| GSQ IQ2_XS, fit 256 | 9.3 tok/s | Sweep, under 15 |

The IQ1_S labs showed weak output, not a memory or speed fault: empty content, a speed run stopping after 18 tokens, and `</think>` in the reply. Probes at 16K: tool code back 5 of 10, context 3 of 5. More reruns could only produce a chance pass, so they were stopped after five.

### Bonsai 2 27B

The PQ2_0 40K recipe stays the best on a trusted image (15.5 tok/s). On trusted prism-b10683, PTQ1_0 with MTP gains nothing: 13.3 at 24K, 12.9 at 16K, 13.7 at 8K, against 13.4 without MTP.

A newer Prism ML fork release may be faster once a trusted image wraps it.
