# RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU: A2000 launches for Qwen3.6 35B-A3B and Ornith 1.5 35B-A3B

> Tuning notes, in the order the work happened. Many recipes, launches, figures and `needs` values here were replaced
> later. The recipes in this PR and their final figures are in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.md`.

Hardware, driver and method are as in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261103.md`. Runs marked "laptop 2" used a second,
identical ThinkPad P1 Gen 5. Every engine container was capped at 27 GB (`--memory`, `--memory-swap`). `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261104.memwatch-ornith-1.5-35b-a3b-laptop1.csv` logs free RAM during that run. On the second laptop `tools/memwatch.sh` recorded only timestamps (`bc` was not installed), so those logs are left out; RAM figures for those labs come from the lab runner's MemAvailable readings in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.runlabs.log`.

## Launches

The rtx-5070-12gb autofit launches keep VRAM free for a desktop and draft 3 MTP tokens. This laptop runs its
display on the Intel GPU, so the A2000 launches use `--fit-target 512`, which puts more experts on the card, and
MTP draft 1. The Ornith launch also drops the mmproj, so it is text only.

30 s decode runs of the lab speed prompt (two samples, mean):

| Model | Launch | Decode (tok/s) |
|---|---|---:|
| Qwen3.6 35B-A3B | rtx-5070-12gb launch | 18.8 |
| Qwen3.6 35B-A3B | fit 512, draft 3 / 2 / 1 | 21.4 / 23.5 / 24.5 |
| Ornith 1.5 35B-A3B | rtx-5070-12gb launch | 14.2 |
| Ornith 1.5 35B-A3B | text only, fit 512, draft 3 / 2 / 1 | 21.8 / 25.5 / 28.4 |

## Lab results

| Recipe | Machine | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `qwen3.6-35b-a3b.llama.cpp.256k` | laptop 2 | all six | 25.2 tok/s | 214 tok/s | 22.3 GB |
| `ornith-1.5-35b-a3b.llama.cpp.256k` | laptop 1 | all six | 27.8 tok/s | 218 tok/s | 21.4 GB |
| `ornith-1.5-35b-a3b.llama.cpp.256k` | laptop 2 | all six | 27.5 tok/s | 213 tok/s | 20.9 GB |

RAM drop is the fall in MemAvailable from engine start to its lowest point. `needs.host_ram_gb` is 24 for Qwen
and 23 for Ornith. The Qwen recipe now uses the A2000 launch; the 19.2 tok/s run with the rtx-5070-12gb launch is in
the 20261103 notes.

On laptop 2, `bonsai-2-9b.llama.cpp.32k` passed at 45.0 tok/s, and `gemma-4-12b.tabbyapi.32k` decoded 14.5 tok/s
and failed the speed gate (15.1 on laptop 1).

## Bonsai 2 9B at 256K

`llama.cpp-bonsai-2-9b-q2-0-256k-rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb` is the 32K cuda12 launch with the full 262,144-token context
and a q4_0 KV cache (only 8 of 32 layers hold KV). Everything stays on the GPU (6,053 MiB).

| Variant (30 s runs) | Decode (tok/s) | GPU peak |
|---|---:|---:|
| 32K f16 (cuda12 launch) | 45.0 | |
| 128K q8_0 | 45.3 | 5,147 MiB |
| 256K q8_0 | 45.3 | 7,867 MiB |
| 256K q4_0 | 45.4 | 5,819 MiB |

Lab run: all six gates, 44.9 tok/s decode, 459 tok/s prefill, RAM drop 4.0 GB. It replaces the
`bonsai-2-9b.llama.cpp.32k` recipe for this card (45.0 tok/s at 32K).

## Gemma 4 12B on llama.cpp

Google's QAT Q4_0 GGUF fully on the GPU with ggml-org's MTP head as the draft. The mmproj runs on the CPU
(`--no-mmproj-offload`), so vision stays and about 340 MiB of VRAM is freed. All runs on b11146 with q4_0 KV.

| Variant (30 s runs) | Decode (tok/s) |
|---|---:|
| no draft, 32K | 20.7 |
| MTP 1 / 2 / 3 at 48K | 30.2 / 31.3-32.2 / 30.5 |
| MTP 4 at 32K | 30.2 |
| MTP 3 with a Q8_0 draft head | 26.0 |
| MTP 2 at 64K, ubatch 256 | 32.3 |
| MTP 2 at 80K, ubatch 128, q4_0 draft KV | 31.4 |
| MTP 2 at 96K | MTP context does not fit |
| no draft, 128K, ubatch 256 | 20.6 |
| no draft, 160K and up | context does not fit |

| Recipe | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `gemma-4-12b.llama.cpp.80k` | all six | 31.8 tok/s | 392 tok/s | 1.2 GB |
| `gemma-4-12b.llama.cpp.128k` (no draft, ubatch 256) | all six | 20.6 tok/s | 334 tok/s | 1.0 GB |

The `gemma-4-12b.tabbyapi.32k` recipe for this card is removed: 14.5 and 15.1 tok/s on the two laptops, against
31.8 here. TabbyAPI EXL3 at 2.5 bpw reached 15.5 at best.

## Bonsai 2 27B

Prism ML's ternary Qwen3.8-27B, fully on the GPU, text only. The only trusted image for the Prism ML llama.cpp fork
wraps prism-b10683. Decode is compute and power bound on this card (34.9 W, 1,267 MHz).

| Variant (30 s runs) | Decode (tok/s) |
|---|---:|
| PTQ1_0, b10683, 32K and 64K | 13.4 |
| PTQ1_0 + Bonsai 2 2B draft | does not load on b10683 (Hadamard v2) |
| PQ2_0, b10683, 8K / 16K / 32K / 40K | 15.6 |
| PQ2_0, b10683, 48K and 64K | context does not fit |

| Recipe | Gates | Decode | Prefill | RAM drop |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `bonsai-2-27b.llama.cpp.40k` (PQ2_0, b10683) | all six | 15.5 tok/s | 210 tok/s | 1.7 GB |

A newer Prism ML fork release may be faster once a trusted image wraps it.
