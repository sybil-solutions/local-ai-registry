# RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU: final recipes, 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-08

Hardware, driver and method are as in `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261103.md`. Every lab in the runner log ran on the second, identical laptop
(ThinkPad P1 Gen 5, i7-12700H, 32 GB single-channel DDR5-4800, RTX A2000 8GB Laptop GPU at the default 35 W, no power or clock changes).

## How the labs ran

Each lab is `lab/lab.py try --on endpoint` against an engine started with `docker run` exactly as `lab.py try --dry-run` renders the
launch, published on `127.0.0.1`. The container had no swap and a memory cap (27 GB until 2026-11-06, 30 GiB after), and a watchdog would
have stopped the engine if MemAvailable stayed under 1 GB for 5 seconds. The watchdog never fired and no engine was OOM-killed.
`rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261107.runlabs.log` is the runner's log for 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-08: start, ready, the lab's gate line and the
MemAvailable before and at its lowest for every lab, failures included. `tools/memwatch.sh` recorded only timestamps on the second laptop
(`bc` was not installed), so those CSVs are not included; the RAM figures below come from the runner log.

## Recipes

The main recipe of each model is its longest window that passed all six gates at 16.5 tok/s or more; Bonsai 2 27B (15.5 tok/s) is the one
recipe under that, and the other exceptions are under "Where each model stops". A shorter window on the same file is kept only when it
decodes at least 5% faster; the `.q4-0` recipes and the other shorter windows on a different file are less compressed files, kept as quality options.
Decode and prefill are the lab's figures. RAM drop is the largest fall in MemAvailable during a lab of that launch; memory-mapped weights in
the page cache do not count towards it, so `needs.host_ram_gb` is set above it (see the Welp launch).

## Where each model stops

- Gemma 4 12B: 256K failed the context gate four times (IQ3_XXS with and without MTP, all on the GPU and with `--fit-target` 64 and 256):
  the server returned HTTP 500 because llama.cpp could not parse the model's answer as Gemma 4 output. 252K failed the same way, so
  248K is the longest window.
  The QAT Q4_0 file at 256K with `--fit-target 64` swept 14.7 tok/s, under the speed gate. With the MTP draft it did not start at
  `--fit-target` 64 (no room left for the draft) or 512 (the MTP context failed), and at 1024 it swept 14.8 tok/s.
- Nanbeige4.2-3B: everything on the GPU tops out at 98K. Longer windows use `--fit-target 64` on the 12 performance-core threads. 144K
  passed with ubatch 128; with ubatch 256 its context answer timed out. 152K swept 16.0 to 16.3 tok/s (ubatch 64 or 128, `--fit-target` 16, 32
  or 64, 6 or 12 threads), and a full lab at `--fit-target 32` passed all six gates at 16.3 tok/s, under the
  16.5 tok/s target set for these recipes, so 144K stays. 160K swept 14.4 to 14.9, under the speed gate.
- Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B: 96K passed. Context probes on the earlier UD-Q3_K_XL file found the code 10 of 10 times at 64K, 9 of 10
  at 80K, 7 of 10 at 96K, 7 of 14 at 128K and 0 of 10 at 160K, and a Q3_K_S lab failed the context gate at 96K, so a rerun at 96K can fail.
- LFM2.5-2.6B: 64K. A 72K lab passed, but the code came back in only 2 of 4 context probes there.
- LFM2.5-8B-A1B: Q4_0 at 128K failed two labs (context, then tools and context), so the 125K recipe stays on Q4_K_M.
- Granite 4.2 8B: Q3_K_S at 84K (the engine exited during the context gate) and Q3_K_L at 96K (tools) failed; Q2_K at 96K and 128K passed.
- Bonsai 2 27B: 40K on the trusted prism-b10683 image; 48K and longer do not fit.
- Qwen3.8-27B: no recipe (see `rtx-a2000-laptop-8gb-20261105.md`).
- Dropped because a longer window on the same file is as fast or less than 5% slower: Gemma 160K (26.3), 192K (26.1), 224K (25.8) and
  240K (27.0) next to 248K (26.5), Groot 32K (41.3) next to 128K (39.5), and Ornith SGLang 64K (17.5) next to 256K (17.4). All passed;
  their evidence stays in `lab/runs`.
